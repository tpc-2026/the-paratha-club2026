GOOD TO KNOW

FAQs.

Everything you need to know before joining the club.

How do I order?

Use our order page to choose your box and flavours. Once you checkout you will receive a text and an email confirming your order.

Are the parathas cooked fresh?

Yes. Our aim is to prepare and cook your parathas fresh so they arrive ready to enjoy.

What flavours can I choose?

Our available flavours will be shown during ordering. Club Box customers can choose up to two flavours, while Family Club Box customers can choose up to three.

Do you cater for allergies?

We prepare food in a domestic kitchen where multiple allergens are handled. Please contact us before ordering if you have a food allergy. We may be unable to safely accommodate severe allergies.

Do you deliver?

Delivery and collection details will be confirmed when the ordering area is finalised.

Will the seasonal menu change?

Yes. Seasonal specials are intended to rotate, giving us room to bring in traditional favourites throughout the year.
