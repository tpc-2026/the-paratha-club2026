OUR STORY

Made from
the heart.

The Paratha Club is about bringing a proper homemade Indian favourite into the modern day — without losing what makes it special.

“The best food is the food that makes you feel at home.”

We believe a paratha should be flaky on the outside, full of flavour inside and cooked fresh enough that you can hear the sizzle.

Our menu is deliberately focused. A few things done properly, with traditional flavours, generous portions and the feeling of sharing food with people you love.

And because every club needs a good gathering, our boxes are made for sharing.
